How drag and double click work, and games to practice everything without touching the mouse. The shortcuts stay on the side while you scroll.

Dragging

Pick where the drag starts, pick where it ends. keeby holds the button down for you in between. Shift Alt 4 / 5 / 6 drag with the left / middle / right button.

Step by step

  1. Press Shift + Alt + Num 4. The overlay shows labels on everything it found.
  2. Let go of Shift and Alt. Keys only reach keeby once every key is released.
  3. Type the label of where the drag should start. A red caret marks the spot and the labels come back.
  4. Type the label of where the drag should end.
  5. keeby presses the button at the start, moves to the end and releases. Done.

Made a mistake? Backspace goes back one step inside the current pick (e.g. from split back to tile). It can't undo the start once it's set, so if the start is wrong press Esc and begin again.

Where exactly it grabs

In floating mode (labels on detected things) the start is the left edge of the first target and the end is the right edge of the second. That's why picking two words selects both words completely.

In tile and split modes both points are the center of what you picked, which is better for dragging things like sliders, files or window borders.

Dragging in split mode

Arrows cut the area in half toward that side. Enter or Space confirms the pick. For a drag you confirm twice: once for the start, once for the end.

From the terminal

Add -d (or --drag) to any mode chain:

keeby point -d -o modes=floating,click

The drag uses mode_click.button (left, middle, right). In split mode, ending a pick with g / h / b picks the button instead, and Enter means left. The start marker can be changed in keeby config (the Drag section, with a live preview) or in ~/.config/keeby/config.conf: mode_drag.start_marker_shape (caret, circle, rectangle), mode_drag.start_marker_color and mode_drag.start_marker_size.

Double click

Two ways to get one:

KeysWhat happens
Ctrl Shift Alt Num 4 / 5 / 6floating labels, the pick gets a left / middle / right double click
Shift g / h / b inside split or bisectleft / right / middle double click on the spot you aimed at

The second way lets you decide late: aim with Alt 3, then press g for one click or Shift g for two.
